4. Firebase Services

Our application uses Google Firebase services, including:

  • Firebase Firestore: Used to store and retrieve application data, including market rates and related information.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Used to deliver notifications. A Firebase registration token may be processed to route notifications to your device.

These services may process technical information such as device details, app activity, app-instance identifiers and notification registration tokens, depending on their configuration and your use of the app. Firebase Cloud Messaging uses registration tokens to route notifications to app installations. 6. Subscriptions and Payments

The app may offer premium features through paid subscriptions. Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Google Play Billing handles payment credentials and provides purchase and subscription information needed to verify purchases and enable premium features. The inspected BillingManager code receives purchase information, uses purchase tokens to acknowledge purchases through Google Play Billing, and stores the purchase token, selected plan ID and premium status in the app's private local preferences. The inspected BillingManager code does not show purchase tokens being sent to the developer's own server or Firebase.

We do not receive your complete payment-card details through Google Play Billing.

You can manage or cancel your subscriptions through your Google Play account. For more information, visit Google Play Subscription Help.
